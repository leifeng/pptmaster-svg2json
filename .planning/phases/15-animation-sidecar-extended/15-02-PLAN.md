**(c) 新增 `resolveEffectiveRow` 纯函数:**
- 签名:`export function resolveEffectiveRow(row: AnimationsSidecarRow, slideDefaults: AnimationsSidecarSlide | undefined, deckDefaults: AnimationsSidecar['defaults'] | undefined): ResolvedRow`
- 实现规则(每字段独立 fallback):

  - `duration = row.duration ?? slideDefaults?.animation?.duration ?? deckDefaults?.animation?.duration ?? 0.45`

  - `trigger = row.trigger_shape !== undefined ? 'on-click' : (row.trigger ?? slideDefaults?.animation?.trigger ?? deckDefaults?.animation?.trigger ?? 'after-previous')`

  - `order = row.order`
- 函数体不得捕获任何闭包变量(无 `opts`、无 `entry`、无 `slide` 引用),只接收三个入参并返回新对象。

**(d) 替换 `injectAnimations` L282-290 的内联闭包:**
